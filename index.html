12 / Partnerships and lawful buyer acquisition

Build relationships.
Not unlicensed databases.

Your owner-data idea has commercial logic—past real-estate purchase is a possible signal of interest—but it is not a licence to obtain or reuse personal ownership data. Convert the thesis into compliant partnerships and permission-based acquisition.

Operating principle

Access to an audience is not ownership of its data.

Launch partner campaigns through approved introductions, audience-safe placements and explicit opt-in. Oman updated its personal data law in 2026, including the requirement for explicit consent for commercial marketing. Obtain legal review for Oman and each outbound market before activation.

The agency distributes.
LEO originates.

The new growth programme should be distinguishable from current agency activities and visible in weekly management reporting. Agency performance is useful, but it cannot substitute for internal business development.

  • Cross-border brokers, introducers and partner terms
  • In-house event and investor-preview programme
  • Founder and project editorial narratives

Report internal-origin pipeline separately from agency-generated pipeline.

Main risks and controls
  • Brand confusion: use approved VISTAL and Rove partner language.
  • Weak buyer intent: distinguish awareness from financial and purchase readiness.
  • Data misuse: no scraped owner registries; opt-in and contract-based referrals.
  • Legal eligibility: project-by-project foreign ownership and claims review.
  • Unserviceable demand: staff language and response capacity before scale.
  • Untracked sales: enforce one CRM source taxonomy and stage reporting.
Chairman's decision point / day 90

Scale what produces qualified, attributable transactions. Stop what only produces noise.
